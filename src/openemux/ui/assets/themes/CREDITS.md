# Ready-made theme pictures

Every picture in this folder was drawn for OpenEmux, from shapes, by
`tools/theme_backgrounds.py` (issue 1012). Each `library.svg` is the source and
`library.png` the rendering the app uses. Nothing here is copied, traced or
derived from another project, wallpaper site or desktop: "in the spirit of" a
look means the mood only.

| Theme | Picture | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pastel | sky, clouds and hills | OpenEmux contributors | MIT (the app's) |
| Dracula | night, castle, moon and bats | OpenEmux contributors | MIT (the app's) |
| Kitty | a sleeping cat on a cushion, paw prints | OpenEmux contributors | MIT (the app's) |
| Neon | synthwave sun, mountains and grid | OpenEmux contributors | MIT (the app's) |
| Tokyo Night | night city skyline | OpenEmux contributors | MIT (the app's) |

The color schemes: Dracula (https://draculatheme.com, MIT) and Tokyo Night
(https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme, MIT) are credited in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; Pastel and Neon come from the app's own palettes
(issue 983); Kitty's colors are the app's own.
